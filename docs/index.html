eoview

A fast desktop viewer for satellite and Earth observation data.

Download for Windows Linux and macOS

Sea temperature and ocean winds in autumn 2025, shown in eoview.

See your data right away

Open satellite images from your computer or from the internet, and see them in seconds.

eoview only reads the part of a file that you are looking at. A rough version shows up first and gets sharper as the details arrive. You can move around images of many gigabytes, and the window never freezes while it waits for the disk or the network.

Your graphics card does the drawing, so satellite swaths, map projections and the 3D globe are all just as smooth.

eoview is written in Rust, from the file readers to the interface. It is a single program with nothing else to install, and it uses Direct3D 12 on Windows, Vulkan on Linux and Metal on macOS.

What it opens

GeoTIFF and COG
Including very large files, tiled files and files with overviews.
NetCDF and HDF5
Including files with latitude and longitude grids, like most climate and ocean data.
Zarr
Including the new EOPF products from ESA, and GeoZarr.
Sentinel SAFE
Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2 and Sentinel-3 products, as they come from the Copernicus archive.
JPEG 2000
Including Sentinel-2 images in their original format.
NITF
Radar products in SICD and SIDD.
From where
Your disk, a web server or a cloud bucket. eoview keeps downloaded data in a cache.

A season in 21 images

Give eoview a list of dates and it plays them like a film. These are 21 Sentinel-2 images of the Écrins mountains in the French Alps, from March to August 2025. eoview read them straight from the public archive in Amazon's cloud.

Work with your data

Compare
Link several views together, or swipe, blend, flicker and subtract two images.
Time series
Play many dates on a timeline, and compute averages and other statistics over time.
Measure
Read pixel values, draw lines and areas, and pin points.
Calculate
Combine bands or whole images with formulas, even from different products.
Python
Run your own scripts and Jupyter notebooks, and show the results as new layers and charts.
Wind
Show wind and currents as arrows or as moving particles.
Share
Export to GeoTIFF, make figures for papers and slides, and make videos.
Projects
Save a whole project in one file. When a file changes on disk, eoview reloads it.

A month of ocean weather

Sea temperature and ocean winds from NOAA, from 10 September to 9 October 2025, on top of NASA's Blue Marble. Watch typhoon Ragasa cross the Philippine Sea, and hurricanes Humberto and Imelda spin between the Bahamas and Bermuda.

The wind is drawn as particles that drift with it, and the globe turns while the days go by.

A year of Arctic sea ice

Daily sea ice maps from EUMETSAT OSI SAF for 2025. The ice grows until March, shrinks to its smallest in September, then freezes again.

The files use a polar grid, and eoview puts them on the globe as they are, with no conversion. A simple formula hides the open water, and the few days with missing satellite data are left out.

Download

eoview is free and open source (MIT or Apache-2.0). It runs on Windows, Linux and macOS, and keeps itself up to date.
